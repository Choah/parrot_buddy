# Yellow Lovebird Visual Reference

External references used for the buddy redesign:

- Rosy-faced lovebird / peach-faced lovebird is a small parrot, roughly 15-17 cm, with a short pointed or wedge-shaped tail.
- Wild-type birds are mostly green with a blue rump and a bright reddish-pink or peach face fading into the chest.
- Peach-faced lovebirds have many captive color mutations, including lutino.
- Orange-face American lutino lovebirds are described as golden yellow, with white primaries and rump, salmon-pink beak and feet, and red eyes.

Design translation:

- Use a round, compact, no-crest silhouette.
- Use a chubby head and body, short wedge tail, and small tucked wings.
- Use golden yellow body feathers.
- Use peach/orange face hood and upper chest, not a random orange oval.
- Use salmon-pink beak and feet.
- Use ruby eyes with white catchlights so the red-eye mutation reads cute rather than scary.
- Keep the app transparent and pet-like by default; only show the guide panel when the user asks for it.
